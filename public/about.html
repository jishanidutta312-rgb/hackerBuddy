OUR MISSION

Empowering Hackers to Build Together

hackerBuddy was built to solve the hardest part of any hackathon: assembling the right squad. We bridge the gap between developers, designers, and domain experts.

🎯

The Problem

Solo developers struggle to complete complex prototypes alone, while teams miss out on winning podiums simply because they lack a crucial skillset like UI/UX or backend infrastructure.

⚡

Our Solution

A zero-friction discovery platform that matches builders based on complementary technical stacks, target hackathons, and timezone availability.

🤝

Community First

From university cohorts to global web3 and AI sprints, we support builders in turning ambitious weekend projects into viable open-source tools and startups.
